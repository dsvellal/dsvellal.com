## Context

The Philips India site leader asked me to improve how the site hired senior engineers.

Each part of the organization hired in its own way. The quality of hires varied, feedback during interviews was collected inconsistently, and people were hired for one team rather than for the company.

## The decision

We redesigned the senior hiring process with a cross-functional team, and measured the result through candidate and hiring-manager NPS.

We wrote the process down, phase by phase, including what evidence to collect before, during and after each interview. Every interviewer had to be trained before joining a panel, and every round ended with written feedback on the behaviors we hire for. We then piloted this on 5 to 10 full interview loops before rolling it out, and brought in bar-raisers, experienced interviewers from outside the hiring team.

## What I did, and what the team did

I led the cross-functional team that redesigned senior hiring at Philips India, and have served as a global hiring bar-raiser at Philips. At Amazon I ran 68 interviews in 16 months. Over the years I have mentored more than 30 engineers who went on to senior technical leadership roles.

I have given more than 150 talks, workshops and training sessions since 2010, at IBM, Exeter, Amazon and Philips and at more than 25 colleges. They range from design patterns and SOLID, behavior-driven development and observability to Scrum workshops at Amazon, clean-code and unit-testing workshops at Philips India, and talks for students on interviews, uncertainty and learning. More recently I have taught: GitHub and Copilot, code craft, GenAI workshops, the 2025 Philips University skill-building program I led, and 27 AI sessions so far in 2026. Since 2015 I have also run a community book drive for rural schools.
