What people said

What I took from it

Exeter made me an owner. On state health insurance exchanges the deadline was public and the evidence had to hold, so I learned to find the root cause before touching the code and to propose a phased clean up instead of a rewrite. Starting TExeter taught me that a community can change how a whole company learns, if someone starts it and keeps showing up. Transformation is not about erasing the past but evolving it with purpose, and I still work that way.
