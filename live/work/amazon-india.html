What I took from it

Amazon taught me to operate at scale. I learned to design for the load you will get, not the load you are asked for, to fix problems at their source, and to write things down so the next engineer does not start from zero. It is also where I started teaching and interviewing seriously: 68 interviews and a question bank used across hiring events showed me that raising the bar is a daily habit, not a policy.
