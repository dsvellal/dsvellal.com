Coached a SaaS team on reusable CI workflows

  • Delivery
  • Teaching
Without it
Each SaaS team would design its CI pipelines from scratch.
The call
Coach the developers through the design by pairing with them, and let them build the workflows.
What I did
Held four design discussions on the Java workflow flavor, then weekly pair reviews with the developers.
What changed
The project lead made the pair reviews a weekly practice and suggested I share the approach as a best practice for SaaS teams.
