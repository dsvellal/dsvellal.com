Led InnerSource adoption that cut security-scanner setup from months to minutes

I guided an IT platform team off one-off pipelines and onto reusable GitHub workflows any product team could adopt, so wiring in the required security scanner became self-service.

months to minutes

time to wire a project to the required security scanner

Without it
Each product team would keep spending months wiring the mandatory security scanner into its pipeline by hand.
The call
Set a platform model where the platform team offers self-service workflows and product teams adopt them on their own.
What I did
Taught the team how platform teams serve product teams, guided it to build reusable workflows into the SaaS factory pipelines, and demonstrated a workflow that connects a project to the scanner in a few clicks.
What changed
What took months now takes minutes. My manager called it a vision that I implemented and sold to the teams, and those teams now build reusable workflows for everyone.
