Built a regulatory knowledge layer proof of concept

I built a proof of concept that turned 24 regulatory files into 10,985 searchable chunks and a 994-node knowledge graph, and made the case for it to leadership.

  • AI
  • Quality

Without it
Regulatory, design, risk and complaint evidence would stay scattered and be gathered by hand for each investigation.
The call
Normalize the evidence once and reuse it for every investigation draft.
What I did
Built the pipeline, documented the extraction, search and graph tools, and turned the result into a short narrative for leaders.
What changed
In the proof of concept, the corpus regenerated a complete investigation assessment with near-identical coverage to the manual version.
