Led a company-wide software craftsmanship report on 138 projects

As lead author of the State of Software Craftsmanship 2023 report, I led the analysis of data from 138 projects, about 80% of the software community, and linked low practice maturity to a higher chance of product faults.

Without it
Leaders would decide where to invest in quality by opinion, without evidence.
The call
Measure first, then publish the data with clear recommendations for where to invest.
What I did
Led the analysis and writing with co-authors from the Software Excellence team and turned the findings into recommendations for leaders and practitioners.
What changed
The report was published company-wide and praised publicly by the head of software excellence. It showed that about a third of teams did not use gates to enforce quality.
