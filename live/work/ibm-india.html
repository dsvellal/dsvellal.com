What I took from it

IBM is where I learned to build. I owned a component that customers depended on worldwide, filed my first patent, and ran hackdays across India that turned ideas into working software. I learned that the work around the job, the events, talks and inventions, compounds as much as the job itself. I still believe the fastest way to grow is to own something end to end and invite others in.
