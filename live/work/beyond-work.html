What I took from it

Outside work I learned that giving back scales people the way good engineering scales systems. Nine book drives, asking the same friends every year, grew from Rs 37,500 in 2015 to Rs 235,218 in 2026. Standing in front of students, and telling them I failed about 50 interviews, taught me that honesty about failure helps people more than advice about success. I keep doing both because they remind me who the work is for.
