What I took from it

North America is where I learned to lead AI-enabled transformation in regulated software. Trust comes before tooling: listen to what a business needs, show a working result, then scale it, which is how the traceability platform went from a pilot in three businesses to a fourth that chose to adopt it. In regulated work AI earns its place only when a person can see, check and own every decision it touches, so I surface the evidence for a person to judge before automating the judgment. Leading a global team taught me that my job is to make that standard easy for others to hold, not to hold it alone.
