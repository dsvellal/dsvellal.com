Vermont's public deadline, met

Vermont's health insurance exchange needed its change function live by a public deadline. I had triaged the release's open tickets in January, and I stood by through the go-live weekend with about 20 India engineers. The Governor then announced the deadline was met.

  • Delivery
  • Reliability

~20

India engineers on the weekend go-live

Without it
The state would have missed a publicly committed deadline for residents to change their coverage.
The call
Stay on the go-live through the weekend until it worked.
What I did
Sorted the release's open tickets by owner and fix effort in January so scope could be set, then troubleshot live issues through the go-live weekend.
What changed
The change function went live on time, and the product's US leadership and a partner's leadership thanked the India team.
