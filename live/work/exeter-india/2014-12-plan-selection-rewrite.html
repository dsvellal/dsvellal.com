16 Dec 2014Exeter IndiaProject

Leading the plan selection rebuild

I led a team of India developers in rewriting the exchange's plan selection on a layered data design, and presented the demo. The US head of development called it simply amazing.

  • Delivery
  • Quality
  • Reliability
Without it
Plan selection stayed slow and tangled, with every change risky.
The call
Rebuild on layers: a data layer, business entities separated from the CRM system, and proper logging and messaging.
What I did
Set the layered approach, pulled the team's before-and-after measurements into one deck and presented the demo to US leadership.
What changed
The US head of development praised its speed, maintainability, extensibility and logging, and held it up to the organization as quality delivered in a short time.

What people said

Newer: Thanked for six months of leading TExeterOlder: Rated for bringing the India team into the re-architecture
