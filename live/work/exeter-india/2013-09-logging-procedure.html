A logging procedure for every layer

Production failures were being debugged on calls with no information. With the delivery head, I co-wrote a logging procedure for every layer of the exchange platform, and he took it to US leadership to decide on a rollout.

  • Reliability
  • Quality
Firsthand words“We are getting into frustrating calls with no information and I think this document is something that we should start looking at this.”Delivery head, Exeter Software India · 2013
Without it
Support calls kept running without data, and failures stayed hard to locate.
The call
Fix the missing evidence first: agree what each layer must log before debating individual bugs.
What I did
Co-wrote the procedure with the delivery head over a few weeks. My review that quarter noted I was the first on the team to take ownership across layers.
What changed
The delivery head sent the draft to US leadership and asked a US architect to help finish it and decide on a rollout.

What people said

Newer: First review: taking ownership and spreading knowledge
