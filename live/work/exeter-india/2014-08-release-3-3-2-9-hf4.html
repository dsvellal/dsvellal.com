Leading a 400-fix release to its planned date

I led the India developers on a release that cleared the bug backlog with over 400 fixes. The India team did almost all of the work from development to QA, and the release shipped on its planned date.

Without it
Releases without a hard customer date had a record of slipping dates or dropping scope.
The call
Hold the planned date anyway, and use the release to fix the old date-logic problems for good.
What I did
Led the India developers through the fixes and the date-logic rework, then thanked the team and invited them out to celebrate.
What changed
Over 400 fixes and about 80 non-bugs closed. The US head of development called it a bang up job and said the rework would pay big dividends.
