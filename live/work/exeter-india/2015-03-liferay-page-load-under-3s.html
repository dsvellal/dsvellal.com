Pages under three seconds, with the evidence

I took on portal page performance, used user caching to bring exchange pages consistently under about three seconds and proved it with repeatable load tests that I reported to the US head of development.

  • Reliability
  • Quality

~3 s

per page, consistently

load tests with thousands of transactions

Without it
Pages stayed slow under load, with results that were hard to reproduce.
The call
Make the result repeatable: same scripts, same data resets, documented runs.
What I did
Tuned user caching, reran the load tests on clean databases and published the run details and purge scripts.
What changed
Page times held under about three seconds run after run, and the US head of development replied 'Awesome, thanks'.
