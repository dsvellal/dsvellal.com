Proving standard enrollment messaging end to end

I led the proof of concept for sending standard 834 enrollment transactions through an EDI integration platform and demonstrated it to the product and partner teams. A week later, files were processing end to end.

Without it
Enrollment data to insurers depended on a path that had not been proven with the integration platform.
The call
Prove the integration on real transactions within a fixed time box before committing to it.
What I did
Ran the work with the India engineers and demonstrated it live. When integration issues hit, I flagged the delay early and proposed a short-term approach that the US product lead agreed to.
What changed
Files processed end to end, with only a batch job and a file transfer left manual. My review credited me with delivering the work within its time box.
