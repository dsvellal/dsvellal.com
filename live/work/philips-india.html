What I took from it

Philips India is where I learned to build systems for other people. A central team earns its place by going to the teams, measuring honestly and handing them tools they can run themselves, which is how the duplication program and the craftsmanship assessments worked. Hiring and code quality turned out to be the same problem: make the bar explicit, coach people to it and measure the result, which is what the bar-raiser program did across three regions. That belief has shaped every program I have led since.
