Kept outreach going remotely, with about 2,125 participants in seven months

When work went remote in 2020, I moved the connect program online and kept measuring it. Between February and August the sessions reached about 2,125 participants across 12 cities and more than 40 departments.

The call
Move everything online and keep measuring reach monthly.
What I did
Ran and tracked the connects monthly by city, role and department, and reported the numbers for the team's KPIs.

The numbers went into the team's KPI reporting, and the sessions led to three teams going live on quality gates and two adding mutation testing to their pipelines.
