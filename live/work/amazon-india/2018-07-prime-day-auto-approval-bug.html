Caught an auto-approval bug in a fraud rule on Prime Day

Under Prime Day volumes, ruleset failures were letting wallet top-up orders pass without a fraud check; I traced them to one wrongly written rule, pointed to the exact coding error and, after checking with my manager, escalated a sev-2.

  • Fraud and risk
  • Payments
  • Quality
Without it
Top-up orders keep passing without fraud checks through the heaviest traffic of the year.
The call
Go into the rule logic itself and treat the business impact as a sev-2.
What I did
Matched the start of the errors to the time a rule change was pushed, found an assignment where a comparison belonged, and asked the rule owners to correct it on priority.
What changed
The rule was corrected, and my manager called it a great example of deep diving.
