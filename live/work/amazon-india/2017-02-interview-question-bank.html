Without it
Interviewers kept improvising questions, so the bar varied from panel to panel.
The call
Give every panel the same tested questions and answers, so hiring decisions compare like with like.
What I did
Organized the question bash with a colleague at the second site, wrote 10 of the first 20 questions, kept the bank in one template and shared it with leaders travelling to interview.

30-plus questions were used in 14 weekend hiring events and 2 online drives.
