Without it
Thousands of customers stay blocked from creating wallets while an understaffed team works the backlog by hand.
The call
Build a generic bulk tool quickly and let investigators spend their time on the cases themselves.
What I did
Designed and built a command-line bulk action tool, ran it against all 5,927 tasks and accounted for every failure.
What changed
5,293 investigations went back to investigators, the weekly report recorded the delivery as on time, and the tool was later extended for another use case.
