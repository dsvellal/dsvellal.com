About

I have spent my career leading digital transformation in regulated software and systems: helping teams ship faster without letting quality slip, most recently through the responsible use of AI in medical-device engineering where every change has to stand up to an audit.

I studied computer science at RV College of Engineering and joined IBM in 2007. Since then I have worked at Exeter, Amazon and Philips, where I now lead digital transformation from Rochester, Michigan. My focus there is regulated software and systems development, through the responsible use of AI.

Outside work I am a certified yoga instructor and hold an M.Sc. in Yoga, first class with distinction. Since 2015 I have run a book drive for rural schools with colleagues and friends.

My signature combines Devanagari lettering, binary digits and English letters. All three are part of how I write my name.
