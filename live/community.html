Books for rural classrooms.

In 2026, friends and colleagues made 22 contributions to buy books for rural schoolchildren. I first asked for their help in 2015. This is the record of our nine drives together.

What each of us did

I organized the drives and collected contributions. In 2026, the contributions went to a charitable trust in Mysuru that buys and distributes the books. In 2015, nine contributions went to Ekal Vidyalaya, which runs one-teacher village schools.
