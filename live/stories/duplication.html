Philips India/2019/Engineering

Giving teams the tools to remove duplicate code

I paired an open-source detector with workshops. Teams could run their own reports and decide what to remove.

18,688

duplicate lines removed by participating teams

Owner-reported in the 2019 review.

The decision

Measure duplication with a free tool teams can run themselves, and pair every report with a hands-on session.

My contribution

Introduced the tool, wrote the installation and usage guides, designed the workshop and the before-and-after reporting template, ran sessions from September 2019 and compiled the results for leadership.

What changed

Developers across at least six business units ran the tool and reported back; a senior director asked for a mechanism to remove 23% of duplicate code; teams I influenced removed 18,688 duplicate lines.
