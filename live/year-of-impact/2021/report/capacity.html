How this was counted

meetings with colleagues, not the whole calendar

Source: Datta's own monthly exports of meetings with colleagues (dot-connects, plus other meetings listed in the 2021 exports). They are not the whole calendar.

The exports hold a date and a length but no start time, so there is no hour-of-day, overlap or time-zone analysis; booked hours are the sum of the meetings' lengths.

A meeting is one date and subject. Days are weekdays; leave and holidays are not known, so working days are all weekdays of the year.

Participants' names, emails and managers are not kept; the exports' organiser is reduced to me, the team account or someone else.
