Read chart notes (8)
  • Define · Sets the strategy, standards, frameworks and KPIs that other people execute against.
  • Assess · Studies the current state through assessments, discovery and analysis to find where change pays off.
  • Align · Secures buy-in, sponsorship and funding from business and senior leaders, and agrees scope and timelines with them.
  • Oversee · Supervises delivery day to day: huddles, milestones, blockers, risks and corrective action.
  • Authorize · Reviews and approves solutions and holds the quality bar before work is accepted.
  • Mentor · Coaches and teaches people and communities so capability grows beyond the team.
  • Report · Measures results and reports progress, savings and outcomes to leaders, finance and stakeholders.
  • Evangelize · Builds a network of influence and communities and promotes adoption of practices and tools.
