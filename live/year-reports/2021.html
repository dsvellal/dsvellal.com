Shift left quality (quality-at-desk), 10 Feb · Why did you opt into choosing quality-at-desk? (Can you please help give concrete business examples which pushed you to opt into this?)
We recognized that the time wasted on reworking on several code-quality initiatives was significant. Very often, this resulted into product-quality issues, that demanded costly rework as well. Good examples of these code-quality parameters include the rework on failing unit-tests, decrease in code-coverage, increases in dead-code, increases in duplicated code, increases in Static-Analysis or Compiler warnings, non-adherence to coding-standards, and many more. We felt that we were repeating the efforts on getting our code-quality standards back to where we left them a few weeks / months ago. Hence, we wanted to automate this. This is where the initiative of "Quality At Desk" was created and used thereafter.
