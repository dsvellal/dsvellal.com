Facts from the documents

  • Developer Days India was a Software Excellence event on 2-4 July 2024, per the slide template title page.
  • The event ran at the PIC Bangalore campus (workshop rooms on the 6th floor) with day-by-day plans for common sessions, lightning talks, hands-on workshops and a closing session.
  • The schedule template shows July 2nd opening 'by Rob', two keynotes, lunch with leaders and delegates, four lightning talks and a threat-modelling workshop.
  • July 3rd in the schedule: a Leaders Talk, four lightning talks, and three parallel workshops (Filament, Docker, SBOM). July 4th: two parallel hands-on sessions then a closing session and celebratory lunch.
  • The call for papers ran as a Microsoft Form; the sheet collects title, a teaser under 50 words, submission details under 500 words, a profile picture and a bio.
  • The sheet is dated 21 May 2024 in its file name; submissions total 75 rows from 61 distinct names.
  • A shortlist sheet recorded 14 proposals (8 talks, 6 workshops) with reviewer feedback from Sundaresan, Ajit Rajeshwar, Bharath B and Kitty.
  • Reviewer comments shaped the final programme. One asked to broaden the DHF Automation talk to cover the wider Jama tool chain, and another asked to add a pre-requisite on basic Copilot knowledge to the testing workshop.
  • A separate sheet lists the submissions that did not make it into the programme (50 rows, 48 distinct addresses).
  • Two slide templates were prepared, one for lightning talks and one for hands-on workshops, with the same six slides: title, two content slides, Feedback, Contact Software Excellence, Thank You.
  • The contact slide in both templates points to the mailbox, Viva Engage, GitHub and office hours.
  • Feedback was collected in one combined Microsoft export: 188 responses covering keynotes, lightning talks, workshops and the Leaders Talk, with a 0-10 session question and a 0-10 event question.
  • The event question averaged 9.32 out of 10 over 188 responses; 158 were 9 or 10 and 10 were 6 or below (computed from the cells).
  • Workshops drew the most session responses (109), then lightning talks (62), keynotes (11) and the Leaders Talk (6). The best-rated workshop was 'Test Smarter, Not Harder: GitHub Copilot Workshop for Testing' (23 responses, mean 9.57); the lowest-rated session was the SBOM workshop (19 responses, mean 8.32).
  • Recurring improvement requests in the free text were more time and more hands-on practice in workshops, prerequisites shared in advance, and more seats or registration slots.
