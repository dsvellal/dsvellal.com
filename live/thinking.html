Thinking

Short essays on AI, engineering and leadership, written from what I have actually shipped.

  1. AI that an auditor can follow

    In regulated engineering, the useful question is not whether AI is accurate. It is whether a person can see, check and own every decision it touches.

  2. Before you rewrite it

    A full rewrite feels like the brave choice. Usually it is the risky one. Notes on paying down technical debt while the product keeps shipping.

  3. AI agents need a shared memory they are allowed to use

    As engineering organizations adopt AI agents, each tool is quietly rebuilding its own copy of the same context. There is a better way.

  4. Validation is the product

    Most AI agent frameworks obsess over executing the next step. The ones that survive production obsess over when to stop.
